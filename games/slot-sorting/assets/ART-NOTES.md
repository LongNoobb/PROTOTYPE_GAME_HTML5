# Art assets

`tropical-beach.png` được tạo bằng công cụ ImageGen tích hợp cho project này. Ảnh demo của người dùng là tham chiếu cho hướng màu sắc, vật liệu và bố cục. Máy, đường ống, bóng và UI trong game được dựng bằng code; chúng không được gộp vào ảnh nền.

Prompt cuối dùng cho nền:

> Create a production game background bitmap, portrait aspect ratio 4:5, for a bright polished casual mobile 3D toy ball sorting puzzle. ONLY BACKGROUND, with a completely EMPTY large sandy center covering x=16%-84%, y=8%-90%, ready for a separate game machine composited on top. Art direction: premium glossy rounded clay/plastic tropical beach, vibrant warm apricot yellow sand with subtle large soft lighting gradients, sparse tiny orange flattened pebbles. At very top center only a tiny curving turquoise sea inlet with soft irregular white foam. Cropped chunky coconut palms with thick rounded lime-green leaves enter from TOP LEFT and TOP RIGHT corners; small smooth coral-pink rounded rocks against outer sides near top 15%-22%. At bottom left edge a small adorable glossy cyan jelly creature with two large white googly eyes looks toward center from behind a cropped glossy pink rounded toy construction brick with round studs. At bottom right edge a corresponding cute red jelly creature behind a glossy lavender purple toy brick. Bright green rounded foliage crops into bottom outer corners. All decorations restricted to outer edges, spacious sand center. Sophisticated beautiful pre-rendered 3D game aesthetic, soft studio highlights, dimensional smooth materials, soft ambient occlusion, vivid cheerful saturated color, high polish. Front view with very slight top-down perspective. Do not include a machine, pipes, balls, containers, bins, buttons, interface, labels, logos, watermarks or text. Match a toy casual game screenshot, not a natural photographic beach.
